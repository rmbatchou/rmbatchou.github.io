To better understand the model’s strengths and weaknesses, I created detailed visualizations of its performance. 

Key Visualizations: 
-	Bar Plots: Compared actual vs. predicted GFR across age groups, highlighting discrepancies in younger populations. 
-	Residual Plots: Showed that while residuals were smaller for older patients, younger patients experienced more significant prediction errors.
