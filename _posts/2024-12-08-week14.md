---

This week, I conducted a feature importance analysis to understand which variables contributed most to model performance. Random Forest’s built-in feature importance metric was particularly useful. 

Key Findings: 
-	GFR was consistently the most critical predictor across all models. 
-	Functional status and gender showed moderate importance, but their influence varied by cohort. 
-	Age played a more complex role, requiring interaction with other features for better predictions.
