This week, I focused on using the Random Forest model to predict GFR values. The dataset was split into training and testing subsets, with the model trained on key variables like age, gender, and functional status. 

Key Results: 
-	The model’s performance improved slightly compared to earlier attempts, with an MSE of 18.75 (down from 21.89). 
-	Younger age groups continued to show underprediction for GFR, indicating a need for age-specific feature engineering.
